Kodachadri Trek Guide: Western Ghats Expedition

Rising to an elevation of 1,343 meters (4,406 ft) within the Mookambika Wildlife Sanctuary in Shivamogga, Kodachadri is one of the premier trekking routes in Karnataka. The trail combines dense tropical rainforests, steep boulder scrambles along waterfall streams, and sweeping ridge walks across the Shola grasslands.

Key Trail Highlights

  • Hidlumane Falls: A multi-tiered waterfall route demanding cautious boulder-hopping through dense tree cover.
  • Sarvajna Peetha: A heritage stone structure situated atop the summit ridge where Adi Shankaracharya meditated.
  • Western Ghats Ridge: Expansive 360-degree vistas overlooking dense valleys and the Arabian Sea horizon line.
